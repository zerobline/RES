About me

A familiar face, not a formal tutor

My name is Rosie. I am a native English speaker, local to Riehen and a mother of two. I know what it takes for children to feel genuinely at ease with a new adult and how much that matters for learning. Lessons are designed to be fun, interactive and encouraging, helping every child to feel comfortable, motivated and proud of their progress.

Background

Experienced as a Tagesmutter and my work with Basel Children's Trust, used to children of all ages and needs.

My approach

Calm, patient and always kind. Mistakes are part of learning, never something to fear.
